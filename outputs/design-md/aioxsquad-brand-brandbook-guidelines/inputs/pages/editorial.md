## 04 — Manifesto & Positioning — Core Belief

### 8. O MANIFESTO

> "TODO MUNDO ESTA VENDENDO IA. E VOCE? CADE VOCE NESSA HISTORIA?"

## 10 — Logo Don'ts — Usage Rules

### 19. LOGO MISUSE

- ✕ Don't stretch
- ✕ Don't rotate
- ✕ Don't recolor
- ✕ Don't use busy backgrounds
- ✕ Don't blur
- ✕ Don't crowd (POWERED BY AIOX PLATFORM)

### 20. USAGE RULES

**DO**
- Use approved colors only — Accent on dark, dark on light, white on dark
- Maintain clear space — Minimum 1x logo height on all sides
- Use provided files — Always use original SVG/PNG assets

**DON'T**
- Apply effects or filters — No shadows, glows, bevels, or blur
- Alter proportions — No stretching, squishing, or rotating
- Place on busy backgrounds — No gradients, photos, or patterns behind

**CLEAR SPACE** — 1x clear space on all four sides of the AIOX wordmark.
