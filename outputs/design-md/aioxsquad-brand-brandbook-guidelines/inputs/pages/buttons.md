# Buttons — AIOX Brandbook

Source: https://brand.aioxsquad.ai/brandbook/buttons

## 01 — Production Roles

- **SiteCta** — Launch project
- **Button** — Open dialog
- **PrimaryLink** — [Read guideline](https://brand.aioxsquad.ai/brandbook/buttons#guidelines)
- **CtaButton** — [Legacy hero CTA](https://brand.aioxsquad.ai/brandbook/buttons#legacy)

## 02 — Usage Guidance

| Role | Use case |
|---|---|
| `SiteCta` | Primary page or form action for marketing surfaces. |
| `Button` | Generic product and app-shell action primitive. |
| `PrimaryLink` | Inline editorial or navigation action with low visual weight. |
| `CtaButton` | Legacy marketing primitive. Keep in existing sections until intentionally migrated. |
