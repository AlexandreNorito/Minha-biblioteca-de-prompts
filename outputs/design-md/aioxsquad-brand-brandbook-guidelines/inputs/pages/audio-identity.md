## ◤ AUDIO IDENTITY — BRIEF — DIRECTIONAL · NO MUSIC YET

### A squad you can hear.

AIOX sound language is **mechanical**, **mono**, **operator-grade**. Think **Blade Runner console** · **NASA mission ops** · **80s synth**, **not** glossy SaaS ding-dings.

Every cue is ≤ **300ms**, peaks at ≤ **-6 dBFS**, mixed dry (no reverb). Bright, punchy, dry. Monophonic where possible. Always layered with a subtle hiss/grain to feel analog.

## 6 Cues

### 01 · BOOT · SW · Power up

`// 180ms · rising saw · low→mid`
`// used on app load, deploy start`

### 02 · CONFIRM · BLIP · Action OK

`// 90ms · single sine blip · 880Hz`
`// every confirmed action`

### 03 · ALERT · BEEP · Warning

`// 240ms · 2× square wave · 440Hz`
`// errors, rate limits`

### 04 · SWOOSH · SW · Transition

`// 300ms · filtered noise sweep`
`// panel enter/exit, modal`

### 05 · DROP · THK · Heavy click

`// 120ms · low thud + click`
`// drag-drop release, heavy CTA`

### 06 · GHOST · SHH · Ambient bed

`// loop · filtered pink noise`
`// background on live dashboards`

## ◤ SPEC — TECHNICAL

- Peak ceiling: -6 dBFS
- Duration cap: ≤ 300 ms
- Sample rate: 48 kHz · WAV
- Channels: MONO
- Reverb / chorus: NONE
- Fade in/out: 4 ms

## ◤ DO — KEEP IT DRY

- → Square, saw, sine oscillators
- → Analog grain / tape hiss
- → Mechanical tick + thud textures
- → Mono, center-panned
- → Click cue under every tactile action

## ◤ DON'T — AVOID

- → Orchestra hits, cinematic whooshes
- → Glossy casino dings / slot machines
- → Vocal stingers ("yeah!", "nice!")
- → Reverb tails > 200ms
- → Sound on passive states (hover)
