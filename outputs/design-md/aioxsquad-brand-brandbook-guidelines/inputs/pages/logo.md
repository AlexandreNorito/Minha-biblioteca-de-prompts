# Logo — AIOX Brandbook

Source: https://brand.aioxsquad.ai/brandbook/logo

## 01 — Primary Logo

- Primary / Dark Background — `AIOX-White.svg`
- Primary / Light Background — `AIOX-Black.svg`

## 02 — Variants

- Horizontal (`H-AIOX-Squad`)
- Compact
- Favicon / Small

## 03 — Clear Space

Minimum Clear Space = **1× height of "X"** on all sides.

## 04 — Usage Rules

| ✓ Correct | ✗ Incorrect |
|---|---|
| Use approved colors only | Never use non-brand colors |
| Monochrome on brand bg | Never rotate or distort |

## 05 — Color Contexts

| Background | Mark |
|---|---|
| On Black `#000` | White wordmark |
| On Surface `#0F0F11` | White wordmark |
| On Cream | Black wordmark |
| On Lime | Black wordmark |
